# SmartCare V5.2 — Clickable Summary Cards

- Made Maintenance Planner KPI cards clickable: Due/Overdue, Upcoming, Completed, Planned Tasks.
- Made Order Progress KPI cards clickable with grouped status filtering.
- Made Inventory KPI cards clickable for all stock, low stock, and highest stock value.
- Made Overview KPI cards clickable and linked to the relevant Asset or Work Order views.
- Added URL-driven warning filtering to Assets.
- Made Work Order queue statistics clickable to focus individual Kanban columns.
- Made Risks & Issues summary cards clickable to apply status/severity filters.
- Made Project Tasks/Activities/KPI summary cards interactive for useful drill-down/filter actions.
- Added consistent hover, selected, keyboard-focus, and pointer behavior for summary cards across modules.

Existing business logic, authentication, invitation flow, role permissions, and user status behavior are preserved.

- Made Project Budget financial KPI cards open a detailed project-by-project breakdown.
- Made Depreciation financial KPI cards open asset-level breakdowns.
- Made Reports metric boxes clickable to jump to the relevant report details.
